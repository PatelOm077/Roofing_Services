How it started

One pickup, one ladder, and a lot of hail.

My dad, Nils, started Halvorsen Roofing in 2004 with a used pickup and a phone number he answered himself. When neighbors kept asking about their AC, we learned to do that properly too.

I took over in 2018. We’ve grown, but the rules haven’t changed: show people what we found, give them one honest price, and leave the yard cleaner than we found it.

Erik Halvorsen
Owner · second generation

How it started

One pickup, one ladder, and a lot of hail.

My dad, Nils, started Halvorsen Roofing in 2004 with a used pickup and a phone number he answered himself. After a few hail seasons, neighbors kept asking if we could look at their AC while we were up there. So we learned to do that properly too.

I took over in 2018. We've grown, but the rules haven't changed: show people what we found, give them one honest price, and leave the yard cleaner than we found it.

Erik Halvorsen Owner · second generation
